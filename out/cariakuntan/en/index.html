Who We Are and How We Work

We Are Not an Audit FirmCariAkuntan helps you choose licensed audit firms (KAP), accounting firms, valuers and tax consultants. Audit reports are signed by the public accountant of the firm you appoint, under a direct engagement between you and that firm.
Licences Checked Before You PayEvery recommendation comes with its Ministry of Finance licence number, and OJK registration where needed, which you can verify yourself.
Transparent FeesThe first discussion and screening are free. If you choose ongoing support, the retainer is agreed in writing upfront. Partner firms may pay us a referral fee, and we disclose this before you decide.
NDA Before DataWe only ask for full financial statements after an NDA is signed.
